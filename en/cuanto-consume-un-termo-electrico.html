How much electricity does an electric water heater use?

Practical information to measure, calculate and reduce household electricity use.

What matters

Measure representative use, calculate kWh and compare alternatives only after understanding current consumption.

Practical method

Use an energy meter when possible. Actual consumption depends on power, time, efficiency and usage conditions.

Basic formula: kWh = watts ÷ 1000 × hours. Cost = kWh × electricity price.
Last reviewed: September 2026. Third-party prices and availability may change.
