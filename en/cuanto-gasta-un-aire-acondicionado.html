How much does an air conditioner cost to run?

Practical information to measure, calculate and reduce household electricity use. Today’s prices and availability may change.

What matters

Measure representative use before changing equipment. Real consumption depends on power, operating time, efficiency, settings and household habits.

Practical method

  1. Measure actual use when possible.
  2. Convert kWh into an estimated cost.
  3. Compare alternatives using current specifications and prices.
Basic formula: kWh = watts ÷ 1000 × hours. Cost = kWh × electricity price.
Last reviewed: September 2026. Third-party prices and availability may change.
