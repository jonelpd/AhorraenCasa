Is installing solar panels worth it?

It depends on your consumption, what your roof can produce and the installation cost. Before buying, calculate.

The 7 variables to review

VariableWhy it matters
Annual consumptionDefines how much energy you need to cover.
Daytime consumptionThe more consumption matches solar production, the greater direct self-consumption.
Orientation and shadeThey affect available production.
Installed powerDetermines the generation capacity.
Installation priceEssential for calculating the payback period.
SurplusCompensation depends on your contract and supplier.
BatteryCan increase solar use, but also increases the investment.

Do not start by buying panels

First measure your consumption and when you use electricity. Then request several quotes and compare expected production, equipment, warranties, installation, permits and economic conditions.
