How to choose an electricity meter

Practical information to measure, calculate and reduce household electricity use.

What matters

Start with measurement. Real consumption depends on power, operating time, efficiency, settings and habits.

Practical method

  1. Measure representative usage.
  2. Convert kWh into estimated cost.
  3. Compare alternatives after understanding current use.
Prices and availability may change. Check specifications with the seller.
