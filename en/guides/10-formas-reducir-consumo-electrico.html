10 ways to reduce household electricity consumption

Ten practical measures to reduce electricity use before spending money on new devices.

10 practical measures

  1. Measure the appliances you use most.
  2. Avoid leaving equipment on unnecessarily.
  3. Schedule devices when it makes sense.
  4. Review inefficient lighting.
  5. Use sensible heating and cooling settings.
  6. Check standby consumption.
  7. Keep filters and equipment clean.
  8. Compare the real consumption of older appliances.
  9. Review contracted power if your needs have changed.
  10. Use monitoring tools to check whether a measure actually works.
Home automation can help manage lighting, climate control and other equipment through schedules. Automation itself does not guarantee savings.

Practical tip

The basic idea is simple: measure first, calculate second and decide whether you need to buy something.

Conclusion

Start with the changes that cost nothing, then consider products only when the expected benefit is clear.
