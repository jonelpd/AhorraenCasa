How much electricity does a refrigerator use?

A refrigerator runs throughout the day, but its compressor does not necessarily operate at maximum power for 24 hours.

Consumption depends on size, efficiency, temperature, age and usage conditions.

What do you need to calculate it?

Enter appliance power, usage hours, days per month and your approximate price per kWh. Add standby consumption separately if needed.

Tip for a real measurement

When possible, use an energy meter to replace the estimate with your own measured consumption.

Basic formula

Consumption (kWh) = power (W) ÷ 1000 × usage hours.

Cost = consumption (kWh) × electricity price.
