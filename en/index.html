Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator give my exact electricity bill amount?

No. It is an estimate based on power, usage hours and price per kWh. A real bill may include contracted power, taxes, fees and other charges.

Does a smart plug always reduce consumption?

Not necessarily. It can help measure, schedule or avoid certain uses, but savings depend on how it is used.

Does AhorraEnCasa receive commissions for products?
