इस कैलकुलेटर का उपयोग कैसे करें

1. मापें

If possible, measure the appliance with a plug-in meter over a representative period.

2. गणना करें

Enter the power or measured consumption, usage hours and approximate price per kWh.

3. निर्णय लें

If an appliance represents a significant share of your electricity use, consider usage habits, scheduling, maintenance or replacement.

मूल सूत्र

सरल अनुमान के लिए:

kWh = (vatios ÷ 1000) × horas de uso

फिर:

coste = kWh × precio por kWh

Standby consumption is calculated separately and added to the main consumption.
